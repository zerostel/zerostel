# Brand assets

The Zerostel mark is a Z with a hook back to a dot at its start: you can go out, and still come back to where you began. The SVGs are the masters; everything is drawn as paths, with no fonts, images or scripts inside.

## Colours

| Use | Hex |
|---|---|
| Brand orange | `#F1653B` |
| Charcoal: wordmark on light backgrounds, share image background | `#19242D` |
| Light wordmark on dark backgrounds | `#F4F6F8` |

No gradients, shadows or 3D effects.

## Using the logo

Leave about one dot's width of space around it, keep its proportions, and don't recolour, stretch or rotate it. At small sizes use the small icon rather than shrinking the logo with its wordmark.
